# ResumeBot — Product Context

## Product Purpose

ResumeBot is a multi-tool career accelerator: resume optimization, portfolio building, and a multi-region jobs hub for job seekers in India, the Gulf (Dubai/UAE), and Australia/New Zealand. It is a premium SaaS product targeting ambitious early-career and mid-career professionals who want a competitive edge.

## Users

- Primary: Tech job seekers in India (freshers to 5 YOE), Gulf region professionals, and AU/NZ aspirants.
- Secondary: HR teams at companies posting roles and hunting for candidates via email outreach.
- Persona: 24–32 year old software engineer or product professional. Uses the product on a MacBook in the evening or on weekends. Expects polish and speed — they will leave if the UI feels amateur.

## Brand

- Tone: Confident, precise, modern. Never corporate. Never cheerful-startup.
- Anti-references: LinkedIn (blue + corporate density), Indeed (utilitarian/outdated), Naukri (cluttered, dated), bootstrap dashboards, admin panels.
- References: Linear, Raycast, Notion, Vercel — precision, restraint, product-grade motion.
- Register: product (app UI, dashboard, tool — design SERVES the product)

## Strategic Principles

1. Feel like a tool built by engineers who care deeply about craft.
2. Motion is intentional: every animation has a purpose. No decoration.
3. Unified: the jobs hub, tracker, and HR tools feel like one product, not separate screens bolted together.
4. Information-dense but breathable: compact enough to scan quickly, spacious enough to feel premium.
5. Multi-region: India, Dubai/Gulf, AU/NZ — all accessible from one tab system, not separate pages.

## Navigation Structure (Jobs Hub)

Six tabs in the primary navigation:
1. Find Jobs — India jobs feed (default)
2. Job Tracker — Kanban/pipeline for tracking applications
3. HR Emails — table of HR contacts with outreach status
4. Dubai HR — Gulf region HR contacts
5. Gulf Jobs — Gulf region job feed
6. AU & NZ Jobs — Australia and New Zealand jobs feed

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- No LinkedIn blue
- No bootstrap teal (#10B981 hex or equivalent)
- No generic admin-panel aesthetics
- No identical card grids
- No side-stripe accent borders
- No gradient text
- No glassmorphism as decoration
- No excessive bounce/elastic animations
- No uppercase label spam
- No modals as default interaction
